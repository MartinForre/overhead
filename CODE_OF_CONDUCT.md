# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and languages. Keep feedback focused on the work, explain disagreements constructively, and respect other people’s privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, and sharing someone else’s private information without permission are unacceptable in project spaces.

For concerns about conduct in this project, ask a maintainer for a private contact channel. Keep sensitive details out of public issues and pull requests. Use GitHub’s reporting tools for abuse on the platform. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. [Reporting abuse on GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
