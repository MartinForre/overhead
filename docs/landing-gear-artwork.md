# Landing gear artwork

The [landing gear catalog](../assets/aircrafts/landing-gear/catalog.json) pairs ready fuselage illustrations with shared gear sprites. The kit holds the cropped PNG filename, its aspect ratio, the normalized pivot within the sprite, the folded rotation in CSS degrees, and the vertical scale when folded. Each illustration holds an `[x, y, height]` mount for each gear unit. Coordinates are fractions of the original transparent fuselage image, measured from the upper left. The mount marks where the sprite pivot meets the fuselage, and height is a fraction of the fuselage image height. Body gear and retractable tailwheels use the same coordinates. An optional `groundPose` gives taildraggers a nose-up tilt in CSS degrees and a normalized wheel contact height in the tilted image.

Most units sit behind the fuselage. The Dash 8 and P-3 main gear sit in front of the fuselage beneath the engine nacelle, with the top of the sprite clipped at the mount. The Airbus and Boeing planning drawings and the S-92 side view give exterior geometry references. Mounts are calibrated to the poster artwork. Rotation and compression are visual approximations for the side view. ## Aircraft configurations

- The ready commercial aircraft artwork has animated gear wherever the illustrated variant has retractable gear. The wheeled Twin Otter has fixed gear. [Twin Otter configurations](https://dehavilland.com/twin-otter/)
- The AW101, AW139, AW189 and S-92 use animated gear. The illustrated H135, H145, AS350, Robinson R22/R44/R66, Cabri G2, Bell 206/407/412, EC120 and MD 500E have skids. [Airbus helicopter rescue booklets](https://www.airbus.com/en/safety/safety-in-operations/helicopters-safety-in-operations/helicopters-general-safety-information) · [Leonardo AW family](https://helicopters.leonardo.com/documents/16114711/32212934/body_BROCHURE_AWFamily_Mk217.pdf?t=1548263523032)
- In general aviation, the Arrow, Seneca, DA42, DA62, Bonanza, Baron, King Air, PC-12 and Vision Jet have calibrated retractable gear. The Cessna models pictured on wheels, Archer, Cherokee Six, Super Cub, DA20, DA40, SR20, SR22, RV-7 and Ikarus C42 keep their fixed gear in the base illustration. [Aircraft artwork catalog](../assets/aircrafts/artwork.json)
- The military catalog uses animated gear on retractable variants, including the NH90 and MV-22B. The CH-47F, UH-60 and AH-64 depict fixed wheels, and the Bell 412 depicts fixed skids. The Spitfire Mk I animates its main wheels and keeps the fixed tailwheel in the base image. The P-51D animates its mains and tailwheel. [Aircraft artwork catalog](../assets/aircrafts/artwork.json) · [NH90](https://www.airbus.com/en/products-services/helicopters/military-helicopters/nh90) · [RAF Museum Spitfire Mk IA](https://www.rafmuseum.org.uk/london/exhibition-items/supermarine-spitfire-mk-ia/) · [Smithsonian P-51D](https://airandspace.si.edu/collection-objects/north-american-p-51d-30-na-mustang/nasm_A19600300000)
- Floatplane artwork keeps its fixed floats. Amphibious wheels would need mounts on the float or hull and a way to distinguish water and runway landings. Generic artwork uses its base illustration.

When adding a livery, reuse the kit for its configuration and check the mounts against the new PNG. Check the wheel contact points with the gear down and the overlap with the fuselage during retraction.
